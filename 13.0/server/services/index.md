# Services

Services are the entry points of a Web site. A service is usually attached to a URL, and it generates a page.

- [The different types of services](./types.md): services with a path, pathless and attached services, and how Eliom finds the service a request is for.
- [Typing service parameters](./parameters.md): GET and POST parameters, decoded and checked against their types.
- [Service handlers](./handlers.md): the output modules, which decide what a handler returns (HTML pages, redirections, actions, files, OCaml values), and error handling.
- [Creating links and forms](./links.md): links and forms checked against the parameters of the service they lead to.
